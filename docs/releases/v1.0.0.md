RecoveryLens pilot release v1.0: a retrospective description of FEMA Public Assistance across three North Carolina declarations, 4393 (Hurricane Florence, 2018), 4465 (Hurricane Dorian, 2019) and 4543 (February 2020 storms and flooding).

Highlights

- Reviewed storm links: 290 candidate links decided from NOAA narratives (240 supported, 49 rejected, 1 unresolved), replayed on every build against current sources.
- A 98 row county panel: net federal share obligated within 730 days (365 as a check), deobligations kept, 2022 dollars priced by obligation and event year.
- Empirical check: on 2020 counties a ridge model missed funding per resident by $10.06 on average against $8.21 for the median guess. It does not beat the baseline.
- Attribution finding: 89% of the 2020 declaration's money in the window went to projects not coded to a single county.
- Streamlit explorer with county indicators, profiles, release gates with evidence, methodology and CSV download. Main file: dashboard/Home.py.

Limits: North Carolina only; retrospective, not a forecast; no rankings or causal claims; independent interpretation review outstanding.

Full report: docs/pilot-release.md. Case study: docs/case-study.md.
